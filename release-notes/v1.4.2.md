# Release Notes - MedCenter Manager v1.4.2

Version 1.4.2 introduces multi-term patient search filtering, adds the Growth Factors / PRP medical consent template, fixes PDF invoice layout positioning, and enhances modal UI interactions and automated form pre-filling.

---

# Frontend

## Advanced Patient Search

* **Multi-Term Search:** Updated patient search filtering in `PatientsComponent` to split query strings into individual terms. All search terms must match across patient fields (name, last name, second last name, email, DNI), enabling natural multi-word searches (e.g., searching for "Juan Pérez").

## Growth Factors / PRP Consent Form

* **New Consent Template:** Integrated `growth_factors_es.pdf` for Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) / Growth Factors treatment consents.
* **Coordinate Mapping:** Added `growth_factors_es` template definition in `PdfManagerService`, mapping patient identification, age, DNI, legal representative details, procedure description, date, and allergy checkmarks.

## Smart PDF Form Pre-Filling

* **Automated Allergy Detection:** `PatientDetailComponent` now automatically evaluates patient allergy data when populating PDF consent forms, pre-selecting affirmative ("Sí") or negative ("No") options and auto-filling details.
* **Birth Date Formatting:** Automatically formats and injects patient birth dates into consent template forms.

## PDF Invoice Generation Fix

* **Invoice Header Placement:** Fixed layout order in `PdfService` when generating invoice PDFs. Doctor and patient information boxes now render at the top of the document above the itemized billing table instead of below it.

## User Experience & Modals

* **Reorganization of Destructive Actions:** Moved delete buttons from edit modals to view modals for patient notes, medical records, and previous records.
* **Post-Deletion Modal Flow:** View modals now close automatically following successful deletion of items.
* **Unsaved Changes Confirmation:** Refactored dirty-check modal close handling across detail components.

---

# Testing & Quality Assurance

* **End-to-End Testing (Cypress):** Updated Cypress test suites (`patient-records.cy.ts`) to align with relocated delete actions and verified consent template generation workflows.
